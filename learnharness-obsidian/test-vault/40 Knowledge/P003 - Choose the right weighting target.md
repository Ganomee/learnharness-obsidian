# Choose the right weighting target

## Claim
A mean across groups and a rate across all attempts are different questions. Weight group rates equally when the target is the average group. Weight each group by its attempt count when the target is the pooled rate across all attempts.

## Example
For groups with rates 50% and 90%, the equal-group mean is 70%. If the groups contain 2 and 10 attempts, respectively, the pooled attempt rate is (1 + 9) / (2 + 10) = 83⅓%. Neither calculation is universally wrong; each answers a different question.

## Diagnostic question
Ask: "What does one unit of weight represent here: one group, or one attempt?" State the target before choosing a formula.
