## Dialogue

### Turn 1: Range
**Learner:** How should I understand range?
**Teacher:** The range is maximum minus minimum and depends only on the two most extreme observations. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P040 - range]]

### Turn 2: Quartiles
**Learner:** How should I understand quartiles?
**Teacher:** Quartiles divide ordered data into four portions according to a stated quantile convention. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P041 - quartiles]]

### Turn 3: Interquartile range
**Learner:** How should I understand interquartile range?
**Teacher:** The interquartile range is Q3 minus Q1 and describes the spread of the middle half of observations. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P042 - interquartile-range]]

### Turn 4: Five-number summary
**Learner:** How should I understand five-number summary?
**Teacher:** The five-number summary reports minimum, first quartile, median, third quartile, and maximum. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P043 - five-number-summary]]

### Turn 5: Box plot
**Learner:** How should I understand box plot?
**Teacher:** A box plot summarizes quartiles and median; whisker and outlier conventions should be stated because they vary. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P044 - box-plot]]

### Turn 6: Variance
**Learner:** How should I understand variance?
**Teacher:** Variance is the average squared deviation using a specified divisor; its units are the squared units of the data. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P045 - variance]]

### Turn 7: Standard deviation
**Learner:** How should I understand standard deviation?
**Teacher:** Standard deviation is the square root of variance and describes spread in the original measurement units. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P046 - standard-deviation]]

### Turn 8: Z-score
**Learner:** How should I understand z-score?
**Teacher:** A z-score is a value minus a reference mean, divided by a positive reference standard deviation. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P047 - z-score]]

### Turn 9: Percentile rank
**Learner:** How should I understand percentile rank?
**Teacher:** A percentile describes a position in a distribution; conventions differ, especially with small samples and ties. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P048 - percentile-rank]]

### Turn 10: Coefficient of variation
**Learner:** How should I understand coefficient of variation?
**Teacher:** The coefficient of variation is standard deviation divided by mean and is useful only for meaningful ratio-scale values with a suitable nonzero mean. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P049 - coefficient-of-variation]]
