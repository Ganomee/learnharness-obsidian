## Dialogue

### Turn 1: Sample space
**Learner:** How should I understand sample space?
**Teacher:** A sample space lists the possible outcomes of a random process under a chosen description. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P050 - sample-space]]

### Turn 2: Event
**Learner:** How should I understand event?
**Teacher:** An event is a set of outcomes in a sample space, and its probability lies between zero and one. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P051 - event]]

### Turn 3: Complement rule
**Learner:** How should I understand complement rule?
**Teacher:** The probability that an event does not occur is one minus the probability that it occurs. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P052 - complement-rule]]

### Turn 4: Addition rule
**Learner:** How should I understand addition rule?
**Teacher:** For events A and B, P(A or B) equals P(A)+P(B)-P(A and B). The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P053 - addition-rule]]

### Turn 5: Disjoint events
**Learner:** How should I understand disjoint events?
**Teacher:** Disjoint events cannot occur together, so their intersection probability is zero. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P054 - disjoint-events]]

### Turn 6: Conditional probability
**Learner:** How should I understand conditional probability?
**Teacher:** P(A given B) is P(A and B) divided by P(B), provided P(B) is positive. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P055 - conditional-probability]]

### Turn 7: Independence
**Learner:** How should I understand independence?
**Teacher:** Events A and B are independent when learning that one occurred does not change the probability of the other. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P056 - independence]]

### Turn 8: Multiplication rule
**Learner:** How should I understand multiplication rule?
**Teacher:** P(A and B)=P(A)P(B given A); under independence this reduces to P(A)P(B). The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P057 - multiplication-rule]]

### Turn 9: Bayes rule
**Learner:** How should I understand bayes rule?
**Teacher:** Bayes’ rule reverses conditioning by combining a likelihood with prior probabilities and normalizing. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P058 - bayes-rule]]

### Turn 10: Base-rate neglect
**Learner:** How should I understand base-rate neglect?
**Teacher:** A diagnostic result’s meaning depends on prevalence as well as test accuracy; ignoring prevalence can mislead. The linked note records the concept and its conditions: [[40 Knowledge/P059 - base-rate-neglect]]
