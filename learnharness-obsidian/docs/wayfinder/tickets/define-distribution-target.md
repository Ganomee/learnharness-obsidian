## Question

What does “produce a distributable community plugin” mean for this MVP: a reproducible installable bundle with complete source/license notices, or a public Obsidian Community Plugins submission? Which licensing review must be complete before that target is accepted?

Recommended direction: require a reproducible, installable bundle and a completed combined-work/source-notice review; treat public listing and publication as a separate destination unless explicitly required. The upstream is AGPL-3.0-only, and current project status says the integration’s distribution terms remain unresolved.
